📌 General

What is VisualNotes?
VisualNotes is a platform that creates visual, handwritten-style study guides for engineering topics. We transform complex technical concepts in Infrastructure, Cloud, Security, and OSINT into easy-to-understand visual notes that help you learn faster.
Are VisualNotes guides free?
Yes! All VisualNotes guides are 100% free to access online. You can read them directly in your browser with our web-based PDF viewer. No subscription, no paywall, no hidden costs.
What topics do you cover?
We cover four main areas:
  • Infrastructure - DNS, TCP/IP, networking, load balancing, CDN
  • Cloud - AWS, Azure, GCP, serverless, cloud architecture
  • Security - Cybersecurity fundamentals, encryption, HTTPS, OAuth
  • OSINT - Open-source intelligence gathering techniques
New guides are added regularly based on popular demand.
Who creates the visual notes?
Our team of experienced engineers and technical educators craft each visual note. Every guide is researched, designed, and reviewed for accuracy before publication to ensure you're learning from reliable sources.

📚 Learning & Usage

How do visual notes help me learn faster?
Visual notes leverage dual coding theory - combining text with visuals helps your brain process and retain information better. Research shows visual learners retain 65% of information versus 10-20% from text alone. Our handwritten style also makes complex topics feel more approachable and less intimidating.
Who are these guides for?
VisualNotes is perfect for:
  • Students preparing for certifications or exams
  • Early-career engineers building foundational knowledge
  • Solution architects needing quick references
  • Career switchers entering tech from other fields
  • Anyone who learns better with visuals than dense documentation
Can I use these guides for certification prep?
Absolutely! Many users leverage our visual notes as supplementary study materials for AWS, Azure, CompTIA, CISSP, and other certifications. Our guides simplify complex concepts that often confuse beginners. However, we recommend using them alongside official certification materials for comprehensive preparation.
Can I read the guides on mobile?
Yes! Our platform is fully optimized for mobile devices. You can read, zoom, and navigate through all pages directly in your browser on any phone or tablet.
Can I share these guides with my team or students?
You're welcome to share links to our guides with your team, students, or anyone who might benefit. Please share the website URL only. Downloading or redistributing our copyrighted materials is not permitted. All content must be accessed through our official website.

Traditional study guides are often text-heavy and intimidating. VisualNotes uses:
  • Visual diagrams instead of walls of text

  • Beginner-friendly language without unnecessary jargon
