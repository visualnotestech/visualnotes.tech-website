✍️ Write for Us

Share your expertise with thousands of developers, engineers, and security professionals

VisualNotes is building the go-to resource for visual learning in tech. We're looking for expert contributors who can break down complex topics in Infrastructure, Cloud, Security, and OSINT into clear, actionable content.

If you have real-world experience and can explain technical concepts without the jargon, we'd love to hear from you.

🎯 What We're Looking For

We publish in-depth, human-written guides (1,500-3,000 words) on topics like:

  • Infrastructure (DNS, TCP/IP, Load Balancing, CDN)
  • Cloud Computing (AWS, Azure, GCP, Kubernetes)
  • Security (HTTPS, OAuth, Zero Trust, Vulnerabilities)
  • OSINT (Tools, Techniques, Ethical Practices)

Why Write for VisualNotes?

📈 Exposure

Reach thousands of engineers and developers actively learning these topics.

🔗 Backlink

Get a dofollow backlink to your site, portfolio, or LinkedIn in your author bio.

💼 Portfolio

Build your writing portfolio and establish yourself as a subject matter expert.

🤝 Network

Connect with other experts and grow your professional network.

Content Guidelines

✅ We Accept:

  • Original content only – No republished or AI-generated articles
  • Practical, actionable guides – "How to...", "Top X tools...", "X vs Y comparison"
  • Real-world examples – Use cases, code snippets, screenshots
  • Beginner-friendly language – Explain jargon, avoid unnecessary complexity
  • 1,500-3,000 words – Comprehensive but scannable

❌ We Don't Accept:

  • AI-generated content (we can tell)
  • Promotional or sales-focused articles
  • Plagiarized or republished content
  • Thin content under 1,500 words
  • Topics outside our core areas (Infrastructure, Cloud, Security, OSINT)

⚠️ Quality Standards

We maintain high editorial standards. All submissions are reviewed for accuracy, originality, and readability. We may suggest edits or decline articles that don't meet our quality bar.

What You Get

  • ✅ Author bio with headshot, 2-3 sentence bio, and link to your site/LinkedIn
  • ✅ Dofollow backlink to your website or portfolio
  • ✅ Social promotion when your article goes live
  • ✅ Full credit with your name prominently displayed
  • ✅ Editorial support to make your article shine

Submission Process

  1. Pitch your idea – Email us with your proposed topic and a brief outline (3-5 bullet points)
  2. Get approval – We'll respond within 3-5 business days
  3. Write your article – Follow our guidelines and submit via Google Docs or Markdown
  4. Review & edit – We'll provide feedback and work with you on revisions
  5. Publish – Your article goes live with full credit and promotion

Example Topics We'd Love

  • "How to Set Up a CDN in 2026 (Step-by-Step)"
  • "Top 10 Kubernetes Security Best Practices"
  • "AWS Lambda vs Azure Functions: Which Should You Use?"
  • "OSINT for Beginners: 5 Tools to Start With"
  • "Understanding Zero Trust Architecture (Visual Guide)"

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you pay for articles?

Currently, we don't offer monetary compensation. However, you get exposure to thousands of readers, a backlink, and portfolio credit. As we grow, we plan to introduce a contributor program.

How long does the review process take?

We aim to respond to pitches within 3-5 business days and review submitted articles within 1-2 weeks.

Can I republish my article elsewhere?

We require exclusive, original content. You retain copyright, but we ask that you don't republish the same article on other sites. You can, however, share excerpts with a link back to the full article.

What if my pitch is rejected?

We'll provide feedback on why it wasn't a fit and suggest alternative topics if possible. Don't be discouraged—pitch again!
