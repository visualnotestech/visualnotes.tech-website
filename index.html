❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about VisualNotes

What is VisualNotes?

VisualNotes is a visual engineering study platform that provides hand-drawn, easy-to-understand notes on Infrastructure, Cloud, Security, and OSINT topics. Our visual approach helps engineers and students grasp complex technical concepts quickly without spending months on traditional learning methods.

Are the study guides really free?

Yes! All our visual study guides and blog articles are completely free. We believe in making quality technical education accessible to everyone. You can download PDFs, view them online, and share them with your peers at no cost.

How do I access the PDF study guides?

Simply click on any study guide card and the PDF will open directly in your browser. You can view it online, zoom in/out, and download it for offline access. Our guides work on all devices including desktop, tablet, and mobile.

What topics do you cover?

We cover four main areas: Infrastructure (DNS, Inter-VLAN Routing, Netflow, SNMP, STP), Cloud Computing (AWS, Azure, GCP comparisons), Security (Google Dorking, ethical hacking), and OSINT (intelligence gathering tools and techniques). We regularly add new content based on community requests.

How often do you publish new content?

We publish new study guides and blog articles regularly. Subscribe to our newsletter to get instant notifications when we release new content. You'll also receive exclusive tips and early access to upcoming guides.

Can I use these notes for my certification exam?

Absolutely! Our visual notes are perfect for certification preparation. They complement official study materials by providing clear, visual explanations of complex topics. Many students use our guides for CCNA, AWS certifications, and security certifications.

Do you offer personalized learning or tutoring?

Currently, we focus on providing high-quality free resources. However, you can join our WhatsApp channel and Instagram community to ask questions, share insights, and connect with other learners. We're always happy to help!

How can I request a specific topic?

We love hearing from our community! Contact us via email at visualnotes.tech@gmail.com or message us on Instagram or WhatsApp. We prioritize topics based on community demand and create guides that help the most people.
